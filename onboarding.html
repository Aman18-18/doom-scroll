Welcome to Scroll Tracker

A few quick things before you start scrolling.

  • It counts automatically. Open YouTube Shorts or Instagram Reels like normal — every new one you watch is counted once, even if you scroll back to something you've already seen.
  • A small pill appears while you watch. It shows your running count and time spent, and disappears automatically once you leave Shorts/Reels.
  • Drag it anywhere, or double-click to hide it. Your position is remembered. Hiding it brings it back next time you open a new Short or Reel.
  • Check the toolbar icon for a live badge of today's total, and click it any time for a quick summary — or open the full Analytics dashboard for charts, trends, and a shareable weekly recap.
  • You'll get a heads-up if you cross 50, 100, or 150 for the day.
